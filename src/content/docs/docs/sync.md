Your library always lives on your Mac as plain Markdown files, with revisions in a folder beside them. Sync keeps an encrypted copy in one of two places.

## Drafta cloud

Storage through your Drafta account is the default and is what the plan covers: 30 GB, with no limit on the number of notes.

## Your own CouchDB

Connect your own CouchDB instead, locally or on a server you control. It stores with no quota.

## Between your Macs

Each note carries its last-updated time. When two Macs change the same note, the later change wins.

Whichever place you choose, notes are encrypted on your Mac before they are sent — see [Encryption](/docs/encryption/).

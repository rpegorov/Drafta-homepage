## How billing works

Auto-renewal is possible only with a consent that you give at payment (see
clause 5.1 of the Russian offer); you can turn it off in your account. Without
that consent, each billing period (990 ₽ monthly or 9,500 ₽ yearly; $9.99 or
$95.88 outside Russia; paid by MIR, Visa or Mastercard card) is its own order,
so there is nothing to cancel — simply do not pay the next
one and the account moves to read-only mode at the end of the paid period
instead of renewing.

## Refunds

A payment can be refunded within 14 days of the payment date, provided the
app was not used during the paid period.

A charge made by auto-renewal that you did not want is refunded on request
within 14 days of the charge date. A double charge for one period is always
refunded, without a request.

A refund goes back to the card the payment was made with, through the Robokassa
payment service.

## How to request a refund

Write to [support@drafta.org](mailto:support@drafta.org) with your account
email and the payment date.

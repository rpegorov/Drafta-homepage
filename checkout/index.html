Questions

Is there a cheaper plan?
No. One plan, two billing periods — monthly and yearly — with no second tier and no lifetime licence. The storage and note limits are the same either way, and they come from the accounts API.
What happens when the trial ends?
The 30-day trial starts at registration and needs no card. After it, the plan chosen on this page is the one on your account.
What does the app need?
macOS 26.4, on Apple Silicon and Intel. The app comes from the releases page — it is not a web app.

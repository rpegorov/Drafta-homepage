How checkout works right now

1
Pick a plan

The plan and its two billing periods are read from the accounts API when this page loads. If the API cannot be reached, the page falls back to the same plan shipped in the site config — never to a different price.

2
Buying needs a session

An access token, written by the sign-in page, is required. Without it this page sends you to sign in and brings you straight back here.

3
The server answers, this page repeats it

The chosen plan is sent to the accounts API and the response — plan, status, current period end — is printed above exactly as it arrives. Card payment is not wired up yet, so this page never reports a completed purchase on its own.

Questions

Can I pay by card yet?
Not yet. The payment provider is not connected: subscribing records the request on your account, and this page shows exactly what the server returned. Nothing is charged.
Is there a cheaper plan?
No. There is one plan with two billing periods — monthly and yearly — and no other tier and no lifetime licence. The storage and note quota is the same either way and is read from the accounts API.
What happens when the trial ends?
The 30-day trial starts at registration and needs no card. After it ends, the plan chosen on this page is the one on your account.
What does the app need?
macOS 26.4, on Apple Silicon and Intel. The app comes from the releases page — it is not a web app.

Every save becomes a revision — 30 per note, stored on your disk. Open the unified diff, see exactly what changed, restore any version. Nothing is written to your notes without a copy of what was there before.

Local first

Your notes are files, not rows in someone's database.

Drafta keeps the library on your disk: one Markdown file per note, YAML front-matter for tags and status, revisions in a folder beside it. No account stands between you and your own writing, and no format change can strand it.

Because it is plain text, the rest of your toolchain already works: grep across everything, commit the library to git, open a note in VS Code or Obsidian, or cat it in a terminal. Drafta is a native Swift app — there is no Electron runtime underneath and no bundled browser to keep hot.

  • Plain Markdown with YAML front-matter on your disk
  • 30 revisions per note, stored locally
  • No proprietary database, no export-only escape hatch
  • Version control, grep and any other editor keep working
  • Licensed under Apache-2.0

FAQ

Questions worth answering

What does Drafta require?

macOS 26.4 or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel. It is a native Swift and SwiftUI app, not a web app and not an Electron shell.

How do I install it?

Download the DMG from the latest release, open it and drag Drafta.app into Applications. The build is not notarised yet, so the first launch needs right-click → Open → Open; Gatekeeper asks only once.

How do updates work?

They arrive by themselves. Sparkle checks drafta.org/appcast.xml once a day, and Drafta → Check for Updates… does it on demand.

Is there a trial, and does it need a card?

Every new account gets 30 days. No card is required to start it. When it ends you choose one of the three plans.

Where do my notes live?

On your disk, one Markdown file per note with YAML front-matter, revisions in a folder beside them. Nothing about the storage format is proprietary — if you stop using Drafta, your notes still open anywhere.

Do I need an account to use the app?

An account is what starts your trial and carries a paid licence. Your notes are local files either way; the account is how the plan is attached to you, not where your writing is kept.

What does a plan include?

All three plans include the full feature set and the same limits: 10 GiB of sync storage and 100 000 notes. They differ in price and billing period — Pro yearly, Pro monthly, or a one-time Lifetime licence.

What if I use a PC or Linux?

Not yet. macOS is available today; Windows and Linux are planned on Rust and Tauri. It is on the roadmap below rather than in the app.

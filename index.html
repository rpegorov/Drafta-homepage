What's inside

Everything a developer needs.
Nothing they don't.

Built around the tools and workflows developers already love — not another dumbed-down writing app.

CodeMirror 6 editor
The same battle-tested editor that powers Inkdrop and dozens of developer tools. Mature, fast, extensible.
50+ languages
Syntax highlighting for Python, Rust, TypeScript, Go, SQL, YAML, Bash, and 40+ more. Code blocks are first-class citizens.
Split live preview
Editor and rendered Markdown side by side. Synchronized scroll. Resizable divider. Switch modes with a swipe.
Custom themes
Drafta Dark, Tokyo Night, Solarized, Dark Islands — or drop your own JSON theme file. The editor and preview update instantly.
Plain files, iCloud sync
Notes are Markdown files with YAML front-matter. Readable by any editor, forever. iCloud Drive sync — no proprietary format.
Revision history
Every save is versioned. Browse history, view unified diffs, restore any revision. Up to 30 revisions per note, stored locally.
The philosophy

Built differently.

No Electron. Drafta is a native macOS app — Swift, not a browser in a box. It launches instantly, uses a fraction of the RAM, and feels at home on your Mac.

No subscription. You pay once. Your notes don't expire, your features don't disappear, and you don't get a price increase email in March.

No lock-in. Every note is a .md file. Open them in VS Code, Obsidian, or cat. Your data belongs to you, in a format that will work in 20 years.
